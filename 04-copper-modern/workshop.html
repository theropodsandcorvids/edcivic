Building data governance that sticks

A 60-minute virtual workshop with facilitator cues, a slide deck and two participant pathways.

Diagnose a blocker, practice with peers and draft a responsible 30-day action.

Design rationale

The learner
City staff bring different levels of governance experience and limited time.
The approach
Align objectives, practice and assessment; let participants select an appropriate level of support.
The intended outcome
An inspectable facilitation package with contingencies and a clear review handoff.

Objective to assessment

Usefulness ratings measure perceived value. The worksheet provides evidence of learning. Keep the two separate when reviewing the session.

Preview the first page

Choose a learning track

Track A: building foundations

Use when ownership, an inventory or a convening group is missing. Identify one resident problem, the relevant departments and a small, safely approved pilot.

Track B: scaling and optimizing

Use when governance already exists. Diagnose a recurring agreement, equity or transition bottleneck and identify a process that could be standardized.

Delivery and review

The associate prepares the package and logs open questions; the curriculum lead approves instructional changes. A facilitator and producer review the run-of-show before release. These are proposed responsibilities, not completed approvals.

If rooms fail, use chat and individual writing. Offer spoken, written and chat participation; share materials in advance and enable captions. For consecutive interpretation, extend the clinic by eight minutes, debrief by five and transitions by two, for a 75-minute session.
